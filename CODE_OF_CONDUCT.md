# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, welcome different levels of experience, and provide actionable technical feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and sexualized or otherwise inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using a private contact method listed on their GitHub profiles. Do not include personal, student, or credential data in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest when handling a report.
